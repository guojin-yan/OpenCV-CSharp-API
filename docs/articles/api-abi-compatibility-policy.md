# API And ABI Compatibility Policy

The project maintains two reviewable baselines for the current packaged OpenCV revision `5.0.0`:

- Managed API: `compatibility/managed-public-api.txt` and its summary JSON. The exact `net8.0` assembly currently contains 611 public types, 6,300 public/protected members, and 41 namespaces rooted at `JYPPX.OpenCvSharp`. The baseline includes generic constraints and nullable metadata shape.
- Native-to-managed binding map: `compatibility/native-managed-binding-map.txt` and its summary correlate all 2,656 primary native entrypoints with reflected `net10.0` import metadata and checked-in source locations. All 2,656 are required to remain managed-bound; unbound and managed-only counts must remain zero.
- ImgProc upstream map: `compatibility/imgproc-upstream-map.txt`, its structured raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover exactly the 203 declarations emitted from OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/imgproc.hpp`. They classify 167 callable declarations without claiming repository-wide upstream parity.
- ImgCodecs upstream map: `compatibility/imgcodecs-upstream-map.txt`, its structured raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, selected-family inventory, and source-reviewed extension record cover exactly the 39 declarations emitted from OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/imgcodecs.hpp` plus the separately reviewed `ImageCollection` source extension. They classify 22 callable declarations without claiming repository-wide upstream parity.
- VideoIO upstream map: `compatibility/videoio-upstream-map.txt`, its structured raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover exactly the 71 declarations emitted from OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/videoio.hpp`. They classify 40 callable declarations without claiming repository-wide upstream parity. `compatibility/videoio-registry-surface.json` separately binds the 12 reviewed `registry.hpp` operations to 22 native entrypoints and 12 managed members.
- Calib3D upstream map: `compatibility/calib3d-upstream-map.txt`, its structured raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and complete family partition cover the 194 declarations reached through OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/calib3d.hpp` across four source headers. All 167 callable identities are implemented with zero missing or omitted identities, without claiming repository-wide upstream parity.
- Core upstream map: `compatibility/core-upstream-map.txt`, its raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and family inventory cover the 258 declarations reached through OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/core.hpp` across 11 public contributing headers. The 215 callable identities are classified as 176 implemented, 29 intentionally omitted, 5 unsupported, 5 upstream-conditional, and zero missing; this measured slice does not claim repository-wide parity.
- DNN upstream map: `compatibility/dnn-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover 182 declarations reached through OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/dnn.hpp` across three contributing headers. All 159 callable identities carry explicit implementation, omission, unsupported, or conditional classifications with zero unexplained missing rows; this measured slice does not claim repository-wide parity.
- Features upstream map: `compatibility/features-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, selected-family inventory, and source-reviewed extension artifact cover 183 declarations reached through both OpenCV 5.0.0 compatibility headers and implemented by `opencv2/features.hpp`. The 160 callable identities are classified as 134 implemented and 26 intentionally omitted with zero missing rows. The nine `KeyPointsFilter` source declarations remain separate because the official parser does not emit them.
- HighGui upstream map: `compatibility/highgui-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover 33 declarations from OpenCV 5.0.0 `opencv2/highgui.hpp`. The 26 callable identities are classified as 20 implemented, 3 intentionally omitted interactive ROI rows, and 3 Qt-only upstream-conditional rows with zero missing or unsupported rows. Four parser-hidden callback helpers are recorded separately as source-reviewed extensions; this measured slice does not claim repository-wide parity.
- ObjDetect upstream map: `compatibility/objdetect-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover 195 declarations reached through the two ObjDetect compatibility headers and nine OpenCV 5.0.0 public source headers. The 163 callable identities are classified as 153 implemented and 10 intentionally omitted with zero missing rows; this measured slice does not claim repository-wide parity.
- Photo upstream map: `compatibility/photo-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover 145 declarations reached through the two main Photo compatibility headers and three OpenCV 5.0.0 public source headers. All 120 callable identities are implemented with no omissions. CUDA Photo and contrib `xphoto` are separate from this CPU main Photo closure, and this measured slice does not claim repository-wide parity.
- ML upstream map: `compatibility/ml-upstream-map.txt`, its official-parser raw extraction, reviewed classification manifest, summary, and selected-family inventory cover 241 declarations reached through the OpenCV 5.0.0 contrib ML compatibility header. - Tracking upstream map: `compatibility/tracking-upstream-map.txt` and its raw, classification, summary, and family artifacts keep primary `opencv2/tracking.hpp` (10 declarations / 5 callables) separate from public legacy `tracking_legacy.hpp` (25 declarations / 16 callables). ## What The Baseline Proves

The baselines prove deterministic shape and drift detection. The native-to-managed portion is measured and closed only when every primary ABI entrypoint remains bound. The ImgProc map proves its exact structured header slice: 203 declarations, including 167 callable declarations currently classified as 161 implemented, zero missing, and 6 intentionally omitted. The ImgCodecs map proves its exact structured header slice: 39 declarations, including 22 callable declarations classified as 22 implemented, zero missing, and zero omitted; `ImageCollection` is recorded separately because OpenCV's parser does not emit it. The VideoIO map proves its exact structured header slice: 71 declarations, including 40 callable declarations classified as 40 implemented, zero missing, and zero omitted; the 12-operation registry scope remains separately source-reviewed. The Calib3D compatibility closure proves 194 declarations across four source headers, including all 167 callable identities implemented with zero missing or omitted identities. The Core closure proves 258 declarations across 11 public contributing headers and leaves no callable unexplained: 176 are implemented with native and managed evidence, including every parser-emitted FileStorage/FileNode callable, the measured numerical/collection/solver family, and runtime diagnostics/timing through owned UTF-8 results and opaque `TickMeter` handles. The DNN closure proves 182 declarations across three contributing headers and classifies all 159 callables; the selected network, owned-layer, path/buffer model, nested-forward, packed-shape, preprocessing, profiling, and graph-control families require correlated native and managed evidence, while deferred model/NMS/tokenizer/async/plugin rows remain explicit. The Features closure proves 183 declarations and classifies all 160 callables as 134 implemented or 26 intentionally omitted; its ANNIndex family has correlated ownership, matrix-shape, stride, UTF-8 persistence, smoke, test, sample, and documentation evidence, while optional model-backed classes and `KeyPointsFilter` remain explicitly separate. The HighGui closure proves 33 declarations and classifies all 26 callables as 20 implemented, 3 intentionally omitted, or 3 upstream-conditional; its selected families have correlated backend, explicit-length UTF-8, callback registration, exception capture, destruction-order, profile-boundary, smoke, cross-framework test, sample, and guide evidence. The ObjDetect closure proves 195 declarations across nine source headers and classifies all 163 callables as 153 implemented or 10 intentionally omitted. Its selected structured family has correlated native ABI, managed ownership, raw-byte QR, nested collection, chessboard output, smoke, test, sample, and documentation evidence. The Photo closure proves 145 declarations across three source headers and classifies all 120 callables as implemented with no omissions; HDR, CCM, `IntelligentScissorsMB`, TV-L1, and chromatic aberration carry correlated native ABI, managed ownership, state validation, smoke, test, sample, and documentation evidence. The Video closure proves 168 declarations across two source headers and classifies all 145 callables as 138 implemented or 7 intentionally omitted with no missing rows; its selected optical-flow, ECC registration, and main TrackerMIL families have correlated opaque ownership, Mat and point-array outputs, mask/warp/iteration validation, guarded tracker state, native smoke, cross-framework tests, sample, and documentation evidence. The Stitching closure proves 207 declarations across 14 headers and implements all 156 locally supported callables; only the two model-backed LightGlue rows remain explicitly unsupported. These module-scoped results do not claim complete parity with every OpenCV C++ symbol: repository-wide upstream C++ parity remains unmeasured. `compatibility/api-gap-inventory.json` is authoritative for that distinction and for the remaining ownership/error/marshalling audit, compatibility review, and sample coverage work. Each open gap must acquire source evidence, an intentional omission decision, or a focused implementation/test before it can be closed.

`OpenCvSharpBuildInfo.GetNativeOpenCvVersion`, `IsNativeOpenCvVersionCompatible`, and `VerifyNativeOpenCvVersionCompatibility` provide consumer diagnostics for the runtime that was actually loaded. They compare the native numeric and string probes with the managed target version; they supplement package and ABI review rather than replacing it.

Public API changes require a baseline diff, a compatibility decision, focused managed/native regression coverage, and documentation. Native ABI changes additionally require export-audit evidence and an explicit compatibility or removal policy. A package support result never substitutes for an API or ABI review.
